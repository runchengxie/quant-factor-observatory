R&D investment relative to valuation

Public method note · aggregate evidence only · updated 2026-10-04
公开方法说明 · 仅聚合证据 · 更新于 2026-10-04

Research question / 研究问题

This study asks whether companies spending more on research and development (R&D) for their size tend to have better later stock returns. It also checks whether that relationship remains after accounting for size, valuation, leverage, momentum and industry. A high R&D-to-market-value ratio can come from more R&D spending, a lower market value, or both. The evidence below is exploratory and does not show that the pattern can be traded.

这项研究想知道：相对于公司规模，研发投入更多的公司，之后的股票收益是否也更好？研究还会检查，在考虑公司大小、估值、杠杆、动量和行业后，这种关系是否还存在。“研发费用 / 市值”较高，可能因为研发费用高，也可能因为市值低，或两者同时发生。以下证据仍属探索性，尚未证明这种关系能实际交易。

What the corrected results show / 修正后的结果说明什么

The corrected replay aligns each financial input to its announcement date. It contains 77 usable month-end comparisons (77 valid monthly cross-sections in the source) for the next-20-trading-day outcome and 66 for the next-220-day outcome. Each month has a median of about 4,454 and 4,230 eligible stocks, respectively.

修正后的回放按财报公告日期对齐输入。未来 20 个交易日收益有 77 个月末可比较，未来 220 日有 66 个月末可比较。每个月符合条件的股票数中位数分别约为 4,454 和 4,230 只。

Rank IC compares two rankings: the order of companies by an R&D measure and the order by their later stock returns. The table reports the average rank correlation across months as a percentage. For example, 12.81% is a correlation of 0.1281; it is not a 12.81% return. The 220-day windows overlap, so neighboring monthly comparisons share some return days.

What “inverse size” means / “逆规模”是什么意思

Inverse size is negative log market capitalization. A higher score means a smaller company. For example, if two companies each spend CNY 1 billion on R&D, but their market caps are CNY 10 billion and CNY 100 billion, their R&D/market-cap ratios are 10% and 1%. The smaller denominator raises the first company's ratio even though its R&D spending is the same.

逆规模是总市值的负对数，分数越高代表公司市值越小。例如，两家公司研发费用都是 10 亿元，市值分别为 100 亿元和 1,000 亿元，那么研发／市值分别为 10% 和 1%。即使研发投入相同，较小的分母也会让第一家公司的比例更高。

Point-in-time and label rules / PIT 与标签口径

  • TTM means “trailing twelve months.” For year-to-date (YTD) income-statement totals, the estimate is current YTD + prior full-year − prior-year same-period YTD. An annual filing contributes its reported full-year value.
    TTM 指“最近连续 12 个月”。利润表年初至今（YTD）累计值按“本年累计 + 上一完整年度 − 上年同期累计”估算；年报直接采用全年值。
  • PIT means “point in time”: use a filing only after it became public. This replay treats the next calendar day after announcement as the available date, carries the latest filing forward for at most 540 days, and forms the signal at month-end. The 20- and 220-day outcomes use later trading-session prices, before trading costs; the assumed prices are not confirmed fills.
    PIT 指“点时”：财报公开后才能使用。本次回放把公告次日视为可用日，最近一期财报最多沿用 540 天，并在月末形成信号。20 日和 220 日结果按之后的交易日价格计算，未扣交易成本；这些假设价格不代表实际成交。
  • R&D / market cap divides recent R&D expense by stock-market value. The EV measure divides by a proxy built from the debt and cash fields available in the source; it is not a complete enterprise-value calculation. The capitalized-R&D measure accumulates estimated quarterly spending over five years, reducing older spending by an assumed 20% each year.
    “研发 / 市值”是最近 12 个月研发费用除以股票总市值。EV 指标的分母根据来源中可用的债务和现金字段估算，不是完整企业价值。资本化研发指标把估算的季度研发费用按五年累计，假设较早费用每年衰减 20%。
  • The archived financial statements do not preserve every version investors saw at the time. Announcement-date alignment helps avoid using a report before it was public, but does not repair the incomplete revision history. Historical index membership and industry dates also need review.
    归档财报没有保存投资者当时看到的每一个版本。按公告日期对齐可避免提前使用尚未公开的报告，但无法补回不完整的历史修订版本；历史指数成分和行业日期也仍需核查。

Evidence limits and next gate / 证据边界与后续门槛

  • The corrected replay remains exploratory. Historical financial revisions, point-in-time universe and industry dates, market impact, and actual execution are not fully verified.
    修正回放仍属探索性研究。历史财报修订、股票池和行业点时日期、市场冲击及实际成交均未完全核实。
  • A separate fixed Top-200 trading simulation reports 12.70% annualized return, Sharpe 0.57 and maximum drawdown −25.75% across 89 holding periods, using a modeled one-way cost of 25 basis points (0.25%; 25 bp one-way modeled cost in the source). Annualized return expresses a multi-period result on a yearly scale; maximum drawdown is the largest fall from a previous portfolio high. The source does not provide enough detail here to reproduce the Sharpe calculation. This simulation is separate from the ten-group validation; in the source wording, it is “not the registered decile validation or a production portfolio.”
    另一个固定 Top-200 交易模拟报告了 89 个持有区间：年化收益 12.70%、Sharpe 0.57、最大回撤 −25.75%，并假设单边模型成本为 25 个基点（0.25%）。年化收益是把多个区间的结果换算到一年尺度；最大回撤是组合净值从此前高点到之后低点的最大跌幅。来源没有给出足够细节供读者复算 Sharpe。这项模拟与十分组验证分开，不是实际账户或正式运行的组合。
  • The frozen October 2026–September 2027 final-OOS protocol remains blocked from performance reading. No frozen holdout metrics have been produced.
    2026 年 10 月至 2027 年 9 月冻结最终 OOS 协议仍不允许读取绩效，目前没有生成冻结留出指标。
  • An earlier public annual snapshot contains a separate 2019 retrospective reconstruction extension. It is not part of the corrected current-run summary and is not revision-safe.
    较早的公开年度快照含有独立的 2019 年回溯重建扩展；它不属于修正后的当前运行汇总，也不是修订安全证据。

Interpretation / 解读边界：Positive Rank IC is an exploratory association. It does not prove causal R&D alpha, cost-adjusted returns, or production readiness.
正 Rank IC 是探索性相关，不能证明研发 Alpha 的因果性、扣成本收益或生产就绪。
