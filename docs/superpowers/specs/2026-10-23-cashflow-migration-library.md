# Cash-flow migration and research library
## Approved intent
Remove the cash-flow index study from the factor observatory after its public record is deployed by market research.
The user requests a complete migration with no retained source-site page or redirect, plus a more attractive and intuitive market-research directory.
## Design
Market research owns /research/cashflow/replication/. Preserve the published study, four steps, two charts, comparison records, conclusion changes, review dates, source identifiers and original revisions. State that publication has moved; migration is not a new experiment or source revalidation. Integrate the report into cash-flow navigation, search and data/source discovery.
The library groups the existing nine topics into stock characteristics, portfolios and indices, styles and timing, and trading/liquidity. Add anchor navigation and concise card summaries. Keep the critical evidence limit and sample visible, with full conclusions, methods and archives in disclosure.
The observatory removes the study, assets, catalog/series/comparison references, localized briefs and review entry. Old route behaves as a missing study. Other research facts and source hashes remain unchanged.
## Approaches
A redirect-only move would leave duplicate publication ownership; the user explicitly chose complete migration. A new separate top-level topic would fragment cash-flow research, so integrate it as a cash-flow subreport. For the library use grouped cards and jump links instead of a new application/filter system.
## Constraints
Preserve both locales, default English, keyboard operation, mobile/dark readability and research boundaries. Copy only already-public redacted records. Do not claim historical weights, full official replication, executable returns or new source review.
## Integration
Validate provider and consumer revisions together. Merge and deploy market research before removing the observatory record. Use normal reviewed PRs, applicable CI and standing merge/cleanup authorization.

## Baseline changes reviewed
Only catalog hashes for pb-roe, fundamental-family-shadow, hk-fundamental-archive are updated after removing their local cash-flow relationship. The hub hash changes after removing its cash-flow comparison/change entries. Original source hashes, exploration/navigation hashes, evidence values and review dates for all retained studies are unchanged. The removed source-review entry is preserved in the destination publication record.
