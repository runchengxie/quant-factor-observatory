# Reader experience adjustment
## Intent and approved direction
Make published research the first reading path in the factor observatory.
The user approved the proposed consolidation and reader-first presentation on 2026-10-23.
## Design
The homepage loads only the study catalog, highlights three existing evidence summaries, then offers the full study directory and separate factor/data exploration. Cards retain sample scope, limitations and source review warnings. The study directory puts filters and evidence before secondary reading guidance.
## Approaches
A cosmetic-only pass would retain the duplicate reading paths. A wholesale redesign would expand the scope unnecessarily. Use the existing site's visual system and consolidate its content hierarchy, as approved in the conversation.
## Constraints
English remains the default with a persistent Chinese switch. Preserve published evidence, data values, research limitations and stable detail URLs. Support keyboard navigation, mobile layouts and both themes. No changes to research computation or source review baselines.
## Implementation choices
Reuse existing content catalogs and navigation destinations. Keep long method and provenance details reachable through progressive disclosure.
