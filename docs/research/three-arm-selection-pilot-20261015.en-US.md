# The fixed forecast changes one of twenty selected stocks

Research status as of October 15, 2026.

We fixed three selection methods before generating the lists. They use current financials, current financials plus valuation, and those inputs with a fixed financial forecast. All select 20 stocks on an identical eligible population.

The last two lists share 19 stocks in both cohorts. The forecast replaces one portfolio slot under this recipe. A replaced stock could help or hurt; this is not a return comparison or a measure of forecast accuracy.

| Cohort | Initial candidates | Eligible for all three methods | Shared stocks in the last two lists |

| August historical pilot | 5,514 | 5,193 | 19 / 20 |
| October future observation | 5,550 | 5,201 | 19 / 20 |

The historical selections use the financial snapshot actually retained on August 2 and July 31 valuation. Their planned entry and exit are August 3 and August 31. The analysis was designed later, so it remains a post-hoc historical diagnostic. All 60 arm/name records have raw opening-price references on the planned dates, but those references do not prove fills, complete corporate-action accounting or net strategy returns.

The future observations use financial data retained by October 14 at 19:28:20 UTC and that day's valuation. Predictions and selections were locally sealed October 15 at 05:58:19 UTC, before the proposed October 16 entry. All orders are pending. The exit rule is the first qualified A-share session on or after January 16, 2027; the actual session needs an accepted 2027 exchange calendar. Each method retains one 5% slot with unknown recorded industry.

The same rules apply to every method. A suspended entry or upper-limit opening leaves the slot cash without replacement. A suspended or lower-limit exit leaves the holding pending. Unexplained gaps cannot be skipped. Costs, directional execution/liquidity, total-return/corporate-action treatment, eligibility and terminal events still require acceptance. No orders were sent and no new return curve was calculated.

The October financial predictions estimate the still-unobserved Q3 report, even though that financial quarter has already ended. The later stock holding period is the forward observation. The model has not demonstrated an advantage over carrying current values in the complete-quarter financial tests. These fixed selections retain a hypothesis for later observation; they do not select a production strategy.

This is a reviewed aggregate projection of research note `quant-research.experiment.three-arm-selection-pilot-20261015`. Security-level records, private paths, score weights and model parameters are excluded. The complete-quarter financial findings remain in the [preceding study](quarterly-calendar-reconstruction-20261016.en-US.md).
